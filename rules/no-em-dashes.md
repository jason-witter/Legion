# No Em Dashes

Do not use em dashes or en dashes as parenthetical punctuation. Restructure the sentence: use commas, parentheses, semicolons, or split into two sentences. A spaced hyphen is not a violation.

Asimov owns the mechanical pass on prose that leaves the session (`rules/engineer-facing-handoff-scrub.md`), and the dash pattern is one entry in `protocols/asimov-catalog.md`.

Do not reinstate `hooks/block-inbox-em-dashes.sh` or a mandatory pre-paste grep. The residual exposure is surfaces that do not inherit `rules/` (`rules/no-rule-free-forks.md`): route prose through a ruled surface rather than reinstating a character-class check.
